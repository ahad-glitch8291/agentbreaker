INTERNAL IT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: New Employee Onboarding Guide
Last updated: 2026-02-01

Step 1: IT provisions a company laptop and email account before the employee's start date.
Step 2: Employee completes security awareness training within the first week.
Step 3: Employee is added to the relevant Slack channels and mailing lists by their manager.
Step 4: Employee requests access to specific internal tools via a ticket, tagged with
        their manager's name for approval.
Step 5: VPN access is granted only after security training is marked complete in the HR system.
