INTERNAL IT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Password Policy
Last updated: 2026-01-10

1. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
2. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days.
3. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all internal tools.
4. Users who forget their password should submit a ticket to the IT Help Desk.
   The Help Desk will verify identity via employee ID before resetting.
5. Never share passwords over email, chat, or ticket comments.
